Hypothesis Testing Results
--------------------------

T-test (East vs West):
t-statistic = 2.02
p-value = 0.0496
Interpretation: Significant difference in mean sales between East and West regions (p < 0.05).

One-way ANOVA (Regions):
F-statistic = 2.16
p-value = 0.097
Interpretation: No significant difference in mean sales across all regions (p > 0.05).

Two-way ANOVA (Region + Product):
Region effect: F = 1.85, p = 0.144
Product effect: F = 1.81, p = 0.133
Interpretation: Neither region nor product shows a statistically significant effect on sales.

Confidence Interval (95% for Mean Sales):
Range = (103,776.35 , 143,524.61)
Interpretation: True mean sales likely fall within this interval, with ~±20K margin of error.

Summary:
- Regional pairwise differences exist (East vs West), but overall regional averages are consistent.
- Product categories do not significantly differ in average sales.
- Quantity and Price remain the strongest drivers of revenue, confirmed by regression analysis.
